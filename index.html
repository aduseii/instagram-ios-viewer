InstaView

Paste a public Instagram post or Reel. The post stays in the phone screen; Comments shows the caption and a one-tap jump into the real thread.

  1. Copy a public /p/ or /reel/ URL
  2. Paste it and tap View
  3. Switch to Comments

Instagram does not give other apps the full comment list. This viewer shows the caption, likes, and comment count, then opens Instagram for the live thread.
